~ The Ultimate Founder's Guide to Shenzhen ~

🏙️ INTRO: WHY SHENZHEN?

Welcome! 🎉

Shenzhen isn't just a city—it's THE hardware capital of the world! Here's why you should pack your bags:

  • 🏭 Manufacturing Speed: Prototype to production in DAYS, not months
  • 💰 Cost Efficiency: 50-80% cheaper manufacturing than the West
  • 🔌 Component Access: Huaqiangbei has EVERYTHING. Literally everything.
  • 🌐 Ecosystem: Engineers, factories, suppliers all within a 30-min drive
  • 🚀 Speed of Iteration: The "Shenzhen Speed" is real

💡 "Supercharged by hundreds of thousands of engineers, rapid prototyping, unparalleled supply chain flexibility, and sheer scale, Shenzhen is now the go-to city for businesses building robots, drones, smart sensors, and wearable technology." - Peter Diamandis

🗺️ SHENZHEN DISTRICTS - KNOW YOUR NEIGHBORHOODS

Shenzhen is HUGE! Here's what each district is famous for:

District What It's Known For
Futian CBD, Convention Center, Huaqiangbei electronics markets, business meetings
Nanshan Tech HQ (Tencent, DJI), Universities, OCT Loft, startup scene, VCs, tech talent
Luohu Oldest district, HK border crossing, shopping, affordable housing
Bao'an Airport, Foxconn, manufacturing hub, factory visits, mass production
Longhua Foxconn City, electronics manufacturing central
Longgang Huawei HQ, budget housing, residential areas
Pingshan BYD HQ, EV/battery manufacturing, battery tech startups
Guangming Science City, research parks, R&D facilities, emerging tech zone
Yantian Massive port, beaches (Dameisha), shipping/logistics
Dapeng Beautiful beaches, nature reserves, weekend escapes
💡 PRO TIP: Most founders stay in Nanshan (tech vibes, expat-friendly Shekou area) or near Futian/Huaqiangbei (electronics markets). The metro connects everything & Didi rides are super affordable - you don't need a car!
🚇 Key Metro Lines for Founders:
  • Line 1 (Green) - Runs through Nanshan → Futian → Luohu. Hits all the key spots!
  • Line 7 (Blue) - Circles through Futian, great for Huaqiangbei access
  • Line 11 (Purple) - Express to the airport from Futian
🔧 HARDWARE IN SHENZHEN

🏪 HUAQIANGBEI - The Electronics Mecca

Imagine 10 city blocks of nothing but electronics. That's Huaqiangbei.

📍 Key Buildings:
  • SEG Plaza - Components galore (ICs, resistors, capacitors)
  • Huaqiang Electronics World - Finished products & modules
  • Mingtong Digital City - Phones, tablets, repairs
  • Yuanwang Digital Mall - LED & display heaven
